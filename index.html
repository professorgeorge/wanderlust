🧭 Wanderlust — Guide & Installation

✨ The Map Beyond The Directions

Wanderlust is a zero-cost, privacy-first GPS audio companion that automatically whispers local stories, viewpoints, historic markers, waterfalls, and roadside bakeries alongside your drive.

🚀 Quick Start

  1. Mount your phone: Open Wanderlust in your browser or launch it from your home screen.
  2. Tap "Start Journey": Your GPS will track your location and automatically narrate roadside stories hands-free as you approach landmarks.
  3. Or Tap "Plan Route": Input origin and destination to scan highway corridor stops, inspect weather ETAs, and export directly to Google Maps or Apple Maps.

🛠️ All App Options & Features

🗺️ Highway Corridor Trip Planner:
Calculates Fastest vs. Scenic route alternatives. Samples the entire highway corridor every 15–25 km to surface hidden gems, scenic overlooks, historic markers, and local food. Features multi-day daily leg splitting and 1-click export to Google Maps, Apple Maps, GPX files, or Shareable Trip Links.
🎙️ Hands-Free Audio Narration:
Spoken text-to-speech triggers hands-free as you drive. Customize narration depth (Concise Alerts vs. Rich Storytelling) and choose between 4 persona voices (The Wanderer, Local Folklorist, Naturalist & Geologist, Highway Historian) under Settings.
✨ Custom Wonder Pins:
Tap Wonder Pin to drop a personalized roadside secret, campsite, or viewpoint note on the map. Pins are saved locally and included in your multi-device backup.
🚘 OLED Night Driving HUD:
Tap HUD Mode for an ultra-high-contrast dashboard with oversized speedometer, night-time solar badges, and large touch buttons for zero glare.
🌅 Predictive Weather & Golden Hour Sunsets:
Calculates arrival time ETAs at highway checkpoints, matching scenic overlooks with astronomical sunset times and alerting to rain, wind, or adverse weather along the route.
💾 Offline Pre-Caching & Universal Backup:
Pre-cache route corridors into IndexedDB for remote mountain passes with zero cellular signal. Full JSON backup and restore is available under Settings.

📱 How to Install on Mobile & Tablet (PWA)

🍏 Apple iPhone & iPad (Safari):
1. Open the website in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button (square icon with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
🤖 Android Phones & Tablets (Chrome / Brave / Edge):
1. Open the website in Chrome or your browser.
2. Tap the three dots menu (top right).
3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
💻 Desktop & Laptop (Chrome / Edge / Brave):
Click the Install icon on the right side of the address bar to install as a standalone desktop app.
